## Publishing notes

- **Link placement:** keep URLs in the first comment rather than the post body. Edit the guide link
  into the post after the first hour if you prefer.
- **Typography:** the post uses curly apostrophes and curly quotes (`I’m`, `Here’s`, `“REACH”`).
  That is intentional. Some editors silently straighten them on paste — paste into LinkedIn
  directly from the block above and spot-check the first line and the "REACH" line.
- **The "REACH" reply:** you are committing to a manual reply to everyone who comments. Send the
  guide URL — do not ask for an email address in exchange. The guide is genuinely free, and the
  post says so.
- **Do not add:** performance claims, reply-rate numbers, lead counts, or anything implying
  automated lead generation. There is no data behind those claims and the post loses its
  credibility the moment it overreaches. The current copy deliberately says the opposite — that
  access to more information *does not* automatically create a lead-generation system.
- **Do not soften the compliance paragraph.** The lines about cookies, session tokens, login walls,
  and authenticated social accounts are the reason this post is defensible. Keep them.
- **Credit:** the post names Panniantong in the body and links the upstream repository in the first
  comment. Keep both. Agent Reach is MIT-licensed third-party software that TheZaraAI uses — it is
  not a TheZaraAI product. Tag the author only if you can confirm the correct profile; otherwise
  leave the plain-text credit as written.
